# Durable invocation and review evidence design

Draft for separate source/design review, 31 October 2026. No implementation claim. Supplements contracts 14 and 15. Authorized local stand-in checks only; protection and real-provider work remain held.

## Size and sequence

P1/P5 together remain 16–30 active engineering hours, low–medium confidence. Start with a 60–90 minute reservation/recovery slice; inspect and re-estimate after its interruption tests. The orchestrator owns types, config, state, schemas, dispatcher, executor, run and docs. A worker may implement reviewed quota/metadata modules only in an isolated worktree based on the next verified common checkpoint. No recursive delegation.

## Single-writer journal

Optional Mission.invocations has version 1 and entries. Each entry stores jobId, label, role, access, featureId when applicable, reservedAt, selected provider/model/family, optional observed outcome/time, and writing disposition pending/adopted/reverted/uncertain. Non-writing invocations still reserve/count; metadata and skipped helpers do not.

ExecRequest gets controller-owned callbacks for used job IDs, reservation and observation. They close over the exact Mission held by the dispatcher, including TonightStore use. The executor never loads/saves a competing Mission. Before EACH runner.run, including internal retries, check cap and save the unique reservation with incremented agentRuns. No provider child launches if this save fails. Job allocation skips durable reservations even if no log was created. The executor returns actual final jobId and the complete observed invocation list for the outer request; requested identity is not successful evidence.

Observation is persisted before retry, normal return or thrown return. A failed/thrown write is not assumed write-free. A reservation whose observation/adoption is missing blocks complete provenance for that scope. The scratch inflight marker remains until cleanup and durable observation finish; accepted contribution adoption happens later with the committed/test-set subject. Adoption, accepted stage state and clearing pending intent are saved atomically. If that save fails, restore in-memory pending state before the dispatcher error path can save again.

Recovery uses stable durable reservation IDs. Legacy scratch-only markers use a stable hash of label, feature and original timestamp. Persist accounting/recovery keys and uncertainty before clearing scratch. Repeating recovery after a crash must not double count attempts or interruption tallies. Never change an existing pending owner approval. A missing marker does not clear an unresolved durable reservation.

## Retained work

Audit invocations and accepted WorkEvidence are separate. Append each job to WorkEvidence once, with actual observed identity and outcome. Internal failed attempts that may have left retained writes remain contributors; no incomplete/completed duplicate job IDs. Preserve builtBy only for routing.

At accepted commits, retain earlier contributor IDs plus relevant new writes unless a successful reset/restore proves those writes were discarded. Every reset path must reconcile to the exact prior snapshot after Git succeeds; a crash or failed save keeps uncertainty. Test-set snapshots are member-grounded: unchanged retained path/hash carries earlier author IDs; new/changed files carry observed authors that may have written them. Missing mapping stays incomplete. No authorship is inferred from profiles or attempt summaries.

Same-subject retained snapshots gain optional revision and supersedes fields. Legacy single snapshots remain valid. Multiple snapshots require one contiguous chain with a unique terminal; duplicate revisions, forks, gaps, mixed legacy ambiguity or unknown predecessors provide no complete credit. Identical adoption is idempotent. This extends the deliberately conservative preparation reader; tests must precede the extension.

## Exact review subjects and application aggregation

Feature review subject is passingCommit before merge. Capture HEAD and cleanliness before review and after cleanup. Changed HEAD or dirty result cannot receive completed-review credit. Incomplete/malformed output is not completed review; actual identity must be complete. Old or damaged provenance stays unknown. Option A continues work with honest same-family/unknown disclosure, without a new cross-family pause.

Application snapshots at the verified integration SHA retain scaffold contributors and explicit feature references (feature ID, source passingCommit, actual mergeCommit and retained snapshot revision). The controller validates the merge relationship before recording it; the pure reader resolves exact feature scopes, never relabels feature entries as application entries. Missing feature/scaffold/pending evidence makes aggregation incomplete. Known same-family overlap remains disclosed even with incomplete history. Surface summaries are computed before the dashboard's lossy projection.

## Findings and final verification

Finding identity is SHA-256 of UTF-8 JSON.stringify([originFeatureId,severity,file-or-null,description,suggestedTest-or-null]) without string normalization. Retain original text, origin and all accepted assessments. The separate strict final-review schema adds resolutions of known outstanding IDs. Validate multiplicity before applying any assessment: duplicate, unknown, missing, malformed or empty-evidence assessments close nothing for the affected ID.

Capture current verified SHA before final review, and require the reviewed SHA plus unchanged clean worktree after cleanup. Blocking closure also requires passing verification at that exact SHA. Accepted assessment includes actual complete reviewer identity/jobId/time/commit. A later integration change reopens stale closures for current completion while retaining history. No separate paid call is added. Reports say the reviewer reported a concern resolved, not that arbitrary truth was independently proved.

## Allowance coordination

Receipt time comes from the controller and is persisted. Aggregate five_hour and seven_day are both required, no more than ten minutes old, not future-dated, with finite nonnegative utilization and plausible future reset. Omitted reset can inherit valid same-window reset; explicitly malformed reset cannot. Identical readings refresh actual receipt; restored/cached data never gets restamped as a new observation.

Optional metadata refresh is bounded once per candidate provider per admission, abort/deadline aware, with no model count/usage. Codex reuses its existing billing-aware app-server path; Claude has no new bootstrap. Strict mode launches only eligible known candidates, pauses required work with allowance_unknown when none can proceed, skips optional helpers and preserves pending owner approvals. Warn keeps existing limit behavior and explicitly cannot assure the reserve. Existing omitted config means warn; new generated config explicitly stop. Policy is recorded per coordinator invocation, not pinned forever to old missions. Weekly share remains an in-memory per-run baseline.

## Required adversarial checks before acceptance

Retries across families retain writing/authorship; cap reached inside retry prevents the next runner call; crash before log creation cannot reuse job ID. Fail saves before reservation, observation, cleanup and adoption; restart repeatedly and assert one count/tally plus persistent uncertainty. Cover retained failed-rung writes, successful reset narrowing, retained old test members after rewrite, feature repair, stale/dirty review, application scaffold and feature aggregation gaps, unknown/damaged legacy records, finding duplicates/stale commits/failed verification, strict cold start/metadata timeout/stop/deadline, repeated readings and clock rollback. Full core check/build follows integration; historical cloud tests are not evidence.

## Reservation/recovery slice contract for implementation review

This bounded slice records every invocation and prevents double recovery; accepted-contribution adoption, per-test member attribution and public provenance summaries are subsequent work. It must not claim complete provenance merely because reservation/recovery works.

New pure `src/invocations.ts` owns `InvocationJournal` and validation/transaction functions. Mission has optional `invocations`. Version 1 fields: `legacyAgentRuns` (nonnegative safe integer), `entries`, `recoveries`, and `interruptionCounts` (bounded label/count rows). An entry has unique jobId, label, role, access, optional feature/optional flag, explicit scope (feature/application/plan/controller), evidence stage (existing WorkEvidence stages or other), optional exact baseSubject, reservedAt, candidate identity, optional observation {at, actual, outcome}, optional cleanedAt, and writeDisposition (pending/adopted/reverted/uncertain for writers; none for readers). Candidate identity never becomes actual until runner.run was entered. Outcome uses existing RunResult outcomes plus thrown; raw errors/output are not copied into the journal.

All times are canonical UTC ISO instants. Strings are bounded, IDs/control characters rejected, exact known enums validated, identities complete, scope/subject pairs consistent; duplicate job IDs/recovery keys invalid. Fixed storage bounds are 100,000 entries and 100,000 recovery records, independent of today's run cap. Hitting storage bound blocks launches without truncation. Entries cannot disappear when the owner lowers a cap. Count is legacyAgentRuns + entries.length + legacy scratch recoveries (those without a jobId); it must equal Mission.agentRuns. Missing journal initializes legacyAgentRuns from the existing count. Damaged/unsupported present journal blocks a new launch and is retained, not reset. Read-only evidence may classify it unknown.

Executor request accepts optional awaited controller callbacks: usedJobId(id), reserve(job,actualFamily), observe(job,actualFamily,outcome), clean(job). Reserve may return false only for an optional request at its cap. Reserve is before all run logging and runner.run; observed callback is after actual invocation return/throw and before usage/event logging/retry. Each actual call has its own callbacks. Cleanup runs through controller callback after child reaping/Git-settings restoration and observation, before any internal retry; it records cleanedAt before clearing the matching scratch marker. Callback errors propagate as a dedicated InvocationPersistenceError, never swallowed by runner event emitters. Returned ExecResult gains final jobId; earlier observed identities remain in the mission journal. Direct standalone executor tests without callbacks remain compatible, while production dispatcher always supplies them.

The dispatcher closes callbacks over its single authoritative Mission instance. Pure transaction helper clones only journal/count before mutation; save failure restores them and throws InvocationPersistenceError, preventing a later error-handler save from erasing pending uncertainty. Reservation increments count in the same save, then records scratch inflight including jobId. A scratch failure after reservation keeps the durable reserved entry and prevents launch. No outer agentRuns increment remains. Marker creation happens after metadata/admission, never for skipped work. Mission-level persistence errors stop safely before a further runner launch; pending owner approval is unchanged.

Recovery first validates the journal. For every entry without cleanedAt, use recovery key job:<jobId>; for legacy scratch without jobId use SHA-256 of exact JSON [label,feature-or-null,at]. Journal recovery key, one interruption tally increment, uncertain writer disposition and (legacy only) one added count are saved atomically. Scratch interruption state is synchronized to that saved tally, then marker cleared. A replayed key never increments count/tally again. Crash between mission save and scratch clear repeats only synchronization. Marker absent does not discard an unresolved durable entry. Existing scratch tallies are imported as bounded baseline counts on journal initialization; clearing a tally after the existing repeated-interruption decision is itself journaled before scratch synchronization. Optional interruption keeps current behavior. No runtime claim is made that an unobserved writing call wrote nothing.

Call sites initially default explicitly to controller/other until their evidence-producing role is wired with a precise WorkScope/baseSubject in the next slice. Such records count for the cap but cannot support independence. No label parsing creates provenance. Per-invocation frozen-file deltas and restoration/mutation flags will be added before retained snapshots are adopted; readonly cleanup cannot confer completed review credit by itself.

### Exact review refinements accepted before implementation

Reviewer accepted the bounded direction with these binding refinements. Scratch markers with jobId must match their durable job/label/feature/at; unknown or mismatched IDs block as corruption. A stale marker for an already-cleaned entry synchronizes/clears only, never adds an interruption. Every recovery record stores its tally-at-recovery and a handled flag. Unhandled records replay their pending controller decision even if scratch is already gone; only after that decision succeeds does one journal transaction mark handled and, where threshold handling requires it, clear the tally. Thus a crash between effect and acknowledgement safely retries an idempotent effect, while a crash before effect cannot silently skip it.

InvocationPersistenceError joins isMissionLevel and gets an explicit safe stopping path. Waiting-status restoration cannot perform a competing save after that error. Physical child cleanup may still run after observation failure, but cannot mark the entry cleaned or clear its marker. cleanedAt means the controller completed its existing cleanup procedure; it does not prove OS protection or restoration success, especially because existing Git-settings restoration logs some failures. It never grants review credit.

Validation checks canonical chronology, cleaned-only-if-observed, reader/writer disposition combinations, recovery keys and exact job mapping, stored tally and safe integer totals. Metadata corruption blocks even optional calls; only a valid journal at its current cap returns false. Lower caps never invalidate historical records. Separate follow-on retained/review integration must capture known mutation/restoration failures and withhold credit.

### Round1 source-review corrections to the bounded schema

Version1 additionally stores legacyInterruptionCounts and each acknowledged threshold reset as clearedCount:true. Validation replays recoveries in order from that baseline, checks every recorded count and the final interruptionCounts map, and requires recovery time >= observation time. Unknown v1 fields are rejected at bounded envelope/entry/identity/scope/subject/observation/recovery/marker boundaries. This is the unshipped reconstruction schema, not a migration of production records. Recovery scratch save failures use the same persistence-stop path. A validated ID Set per executor request keeps restart ID allocation linear in previous same-label reservations.

Raw logs retain both reproduced failures before correction. Corrected focused141cases/typecheck0 passed; final combined full check/build is running.

Keep other sentences and unaffected items. ## Finding-resolution pure slice contract for review

The root owns new finding-resolution.ts/test.ts; no runtime integration until its pure contract/source review. First60–90minutes, medium confidence. Optional per-feature findingHistory uses {version:1,records:[{id,originFeatureId,finding,assessments:[]}]} with exact original Finding strings. ID is SHA256 UTF8 JSON.stringify([originFeatureId,severity,file-or-null,description,suggestedTest-or-null]). Identical findings deduplicate; whitespace/newlines/order of content remain unchanged. A compatible legacy openFindings list can seed records without inventing any review.

Each accepted assessment stores outcome resolved/open/cannot_judge, nonempty evidence, reviewedCommit, actual complete identity, jobId, at, and verificationPassedAtCommit. Pure helpers never read configuration or infer requested identity. Context must contain a completed review, exact current/reviewed commit equality, unchanged/clean review subject and actual identity; blocking closure additionally requires passing verification at that exact commit. Duplicate raw assessment IDs are counted before shape validation; every occurrence for an ambiguous ID is ignored. Unknown IDs, omissions, malformed outcomes and empty evidence close nothing. Known valid outcomes are appended only for currently outstanding IDs; duplicate replay of identical job/ID/assessment is idempotent. Conflicting replay for the same job/ID is ignored without overwriting prior evidence.

Current-open projection evaluates the latest accepted assessment at the exact current commit; resolved at an old head is open again, audit history unchanged. A minor concern can be reported resolved by a completed review even without executable verification; blocker/major cannot. Pure helpers accept bounded unknown persisted input and return undefined on a damaged/unsupported envelope instead of fabricating an empty history or closure; caller retains original openFindings and damaged bytes. Limits10000records and100000total assessments are independent of today's model cap. Field bounds on evidence/identity/IDs/timestamps/commit and arrays prevent unbounded traversal; original finding text is not normalized. Unknown/duplicate stored records or inconsistent hash/origin/assessment identity make the envelope unusable for closure.

Integration later adds a separate strict finalReview schema with resolutions[], supplies outstanding IDs in gateCPrompt, captures verification SHA before commands and confirms same clean SHA after review, consumes actual final jobId with no fallback, and atomically updates history plus openFindings. It never adds an AI job. Readonly mutation restored by a runner must explicitly disqualify review/closure; the worker is preparing that result flag without changing sandbox selection. Merge changes refresh openFindings so stale closures cannot survive into current completion. Report audit wording states a reviewer reported resolution, not executable proof.

### Finding design refinements accepted before coding

Every pure boundary receives expected originFeatureId and rejects records from another owner even with a self-consistent hash. Context explicitly includes clean:true and unchanged:true; stored verificationPassedAtCommit is the exact verified SHA or null. Blocking resolved records require equality with reviewedCommit. Current projection uses the latest accepted assessment overall in append order, never a backwards search for an earlier same-head resolution. Oversized response arrays reject the whole batch before duplicate counting; all recognizable string IDs participate before row validation. Bounds are10000records,100000assessments,2million total text characters,16000evidence characters per assessment and bounded IDs/commit/timestamps. Exceeding bounds returns unknown/undefined without truncating a supposedly complete result or changing original text. Source review/runtime integration remains pending.

## Pure provenance graph extension: accepted implementation contract

Next bounded worker slice: first 90 minutes, then re-estimate; medium confidence. Only src/provenance.ts and its test are worker-owned, in the isolated reconstruction worktree. No runtime wiring or shared-file edits. Source design accepted by separate gpt-6.1-sol high reviewer on 31 October 2026.

Revision fields must either both be absent as own properties (legacy) or both be valid: positive integer revision, null supersedes for revision 1, otherwise predecessor n-1. Own undefined, mixed legacy/revision snapshots, gaps, forks and duplicate revisions are ambiguous. Validate the whole contiguous chain grouped by exact scope/subject/kind. Direct queries select terminal revision; an application reference selects its exact member, including an older recorded member, only after validating its whole chain. No array/time-order winner.

Application snapshots may carry explicit recorded_baseline {commit: SHA or null} and unique feature refs {featureId,passingCommit,mergeCommit,snapshotRevision: positive integer or null}. Independent controller context supplies expected/observed integration heads, expected baseline, expected feature refs with pending-write flags, all feature evidence envelopes, and whether retained AI scaffold contributions exist. Exact source/merge/revision coverage is required. The later controller must verify merge ancestry and construct expectations from its durable integration ledger, never from the snapshot being checked. A baseline contributes zero authors and cannot excuse missing scaffold evidence unless the controller independently recorded no retained AI scaffold contribution.

Build one bounded job-ID index across mission and all supplied feature envelopes before evidence selection/validation. Count recognizable raw job IDs even in damaged or unsupported envelopes where their bounded arrays can be inspected. Any duplicate poisons full identity and known-family facts for that ID globally, even identical duplicates or entries in unreferenced features. Duplicate feature wrapper IDs or refs are ambiguous, not last-wins. Graph budgets are cumulative and include entries, snapshots, nested job references, frozen members/member authors and expected refs. Coverage wording explicitly means retained mission AI build/fix/scaffold contributions, with test authors disclosed separately; it never claims all authors of a preexisting repository are independent. ### Durable amendment composition (root integration proposal)

Before moving rewritten tests beneath retained code, root records a WorkRestackIntent with exact old-tests/rewrite commits, exact frozen checkpoint selectors when available, complete committed rewrite capture, sorted disjoint changed-path partitions, frozen members and canonical time. The typed record is strict, bounded and tied to the same building feature, saved testsCommit and worktree.
