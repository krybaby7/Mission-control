### Installed-tool manual inspection (not a capability result)

The installed Bubblewrap 0.11.1 manual at `/usr/share/man/man1/bwrap.1.gz` was read without executing a sandbox. It documents an initially empty mount namespace, explicit bind/read-only mounts, private process/network namespaces, a new session, and death-with-parent handling. It also documents that `--unshare-all` includes optional `--unshare-user-try` and `--unshare-cgroup-try`; the proposed required profile must not treat that convenience flag as proof that every required namespace was established.

The manual says its default PID-1 helper waits for remaining processes after the initial application exits. Therefore `--die-with-parent` alone must not be assumed to establish T12's successful-parent-exit cleanup. The fixture/design must exercise this exact case, including detached descendants. A possible `--as-pid-1` approach also changes signal/reaping behavior and is only a candidate until tested.

The read-only-bind and data-bind options do not, by themselves, establish path-race or hard-link safety. The preparation review must inspect how authoritative frozen inputs and writable project paths share underlying objects. No final production recipe is selected by this manual inspection.
